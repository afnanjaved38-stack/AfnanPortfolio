03THE STORY BEHIND DINAARO

BUILT FROM A REAL NEED

From a tool for my own business to a product with wider potential.

Dinaaro began as a project for my own shop. I wanted a better way to manage daily sales, stock, customer balances and business finances.

As I worked on it, I paid attention to the details that make a difference in real retail operations. The system needed to be straightforward for staff, useful for owners and capable of handling more than just basic sales.

I used it in daily operations, tested how it worked and kept improving it around practical needs. That experience helped shape Dinaaro’s approach: software should be easy to use without leaving out the details a business depends on, and it should reflect how businesses actually operate, including retailers in Pakistan and Saudi Arabia.

As the product developed, I saw that its potential went beyond my own shop. I began shaping it into a SaaS product that other retailers could use for their own operations.

That process continues to guide how I develop and improve Dinaaro.

05MY VISION

USEFUL TECHNOLOGY, WIDER REACH

Helping businesses and organizations make better use of technology.

I’ve seen how much work still depends on manual processes, scattered information and tools that don’t fully meet people’s needs. I want to help change that.

My goal is to create and improve software that makes everyday work simpler, keeps information organized and helps people make better decisions. That means looking beyond technology itself and understanding the people, processes and challenges behind each business or organization.

Retail is one area where I’m putting this thinking into practice, but my interests are not limited to one industry or one product. I’m open to meaningful problems across different industries, especially where the right technology can make a lasting difference.

I want to keep working on meaningful problems and build technology that makes a practical difference.
