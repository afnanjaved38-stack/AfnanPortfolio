Retail Platform / Founder

Dinaaro

Retail operations

Running a shop shouldn't mean guessing what is in stock, chasing old customer balances or losing track of where the money went. I built Dinaaro to bring those everyday records together and give retailers a clearer view of their business.

The change behind Dinaaro

  • Moving sales, stock and customer ledgers out of scattered registers.
  • Making expenses, supplier balances and daily activity easier to track.
  • Helping owners understand what is happening across their business.

Education Platform / Founder

EduPulse

Education operations

A busy school day leaves little time for paperwork and repeated follow ups. EduPulse is built around keeping students, teachers and parents connected, making progress easier to follow and learning support easier to reach.

The change behind EduPulse

  • Making school records, results and daily information simpler to manage.
  • Helping parents stay informed about progress without unnecessary visits.
  • Bringing AI supported explanations closer to students who need extra help understanding a topic.

If we work together

Here's what working with me looks like.

Whether you're launching something new or improving an existing business, we'll start with your goals and the result you want to achieve.

Discuss a collaboration
01

You tell me what needs to change.

We'll discuss your idea or challenge, what is holding you back and what a good outcome would look like.

02

We agree on a practical plan.

I'll explain what I can take on, the suggested solution, the scope and what to expect before work begins.

03

We review progress together.

You'll see the solution taking shape, share feedback and check that we're moving toward the outcome we agreed on.

04

We prepare for everyday use.

We'll agree on testing, handover and any support needed, so everyone knows what happens next.

Get to know me

A little about Afnan 😄

Hey, I'm Afnan from Pakistan. I build and run my own business software platforms and work with businesses to turn their ideas and challenges into real solutions.

I've worked on systems that people use in their daily business operations. That experience has taught me that building something is one thing, but making it truly useful is what matters. My background includes higher secondary studies, and much of my practical experience comes from working on real projects.
