# Afnan Javed Brand Kit

## Brand direction
Personal, founder led, practical, thoughtful, and quietly confident. The identity should feel like a real person building useful products, not an agency or a generic software company.

## Logo
- Primary mark: AJ monogram in lime on a deep ink background.
- Source: `favicon.svg`
- In page header and footer, use the same mark from `/favicon.svg`.
- Keep clear space around the mark. Do not stretch, add shadows, or recolor it outside the palette.
- The mark is a compact site identity, not a replacement for the written name “Afnan Javed”.

## Color palette
| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep Ink | `#111820` | Header, hero, contact section, footer, strong text |
| Accent | Fresh Lime | `#C8F36A` | AJ mark, primary buttons, small highlights |
| Main background | Warm Paper | `#F7F6F2` | Main page background |
| Surface | Soft White | `#FFFEFA` | Cards and content surfaces |
| Secondary surface | Pale Sage | `#E8EEE5` | Vision and calm supporting sections |
| Product surface | Mist Green | `#DFE9DF` | Dinaaro preview area |
| Supporting green | Leaf Green | `#5E7F61` | Selected headings and small labels |
| Body text | Muted Slate | `#59635F` | Paragraphs on light backgrounds |
| Fine borders | Quiet Line | `#DFE2DA` | Dividers, card outlines |

## Typography
- Headings: Manrope, weights 500–800.
- Body and interface: DM Sans, weights 400–700.
- Use sentence case for headings and navigation.
- Keep paragraphs short and easy to scan.
- Use uppercase only for small section labels, with generous letter spacing.

## Layout and imagery
- Wide, calm spacing and a clear reading order.
- Dark hero with a portrait on the right on desktop; stacked layout on mobile.
- Use the portrait without adding a fake background.
- Cards should have restrained borders and rounded corners, not heavy shadows.
- Use lime sparingly so actions and key details stand out.

## Voice
- Use simple, direct English.
- Focus on useful work, real needs, practical decisions, and learning through use.
- Avoid inflated claims, jargon, and generic labels such as “visionary entrepreneur”.
- Describe Dinaaro as one important product, not the full limit of Afnan's work.
- Do not publish EduPulse or Raabta in this portfolio until specifically approved.

## UI rules
- Primary action: lime background with deep ink text.
- Secondary action on dark surfaces: transparent background with a quiet border.
- Links must have visible hover and keyboard focus states.
- Preserve reduced-motion support and mobile navigation accessibility.
- Keep the current palette consistent across favicon, header, hero, cards, buttons, and footer.
