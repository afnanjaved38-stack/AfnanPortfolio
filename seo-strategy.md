# Afnan Javed: SEO and Search Visibility Strategy

## Goal

Build a clear, consistent search identity for Afnan Javed. The portfolio should explain his work as a founder focused on business needs and practical technology, while showing Dinaaro and selected client projects as evidence of that work.

## Identity and keyword themes

Primary:
- Afnan Javed

Supporting:
- Afnan Javed founder
- Afnan Javed portfolio
- Afnan Javed business software
- Afnan Javed product planning
- Dinaaro retail management software
- Custom school management system
- Business website projects
- Subscription tracking system

Use natural wording, not repeated keyword lists in visible page copy. Search engines should be able to understand the person, products, projects and relationships from clear content and links.

## Content architecture

- Hero: founder positioning and practical business solutions.
- About Me: building projects since 4th grade, learning by trying ideas, and current focus on product direction, business needs, planning and testing.
- My Work: overall scope and Dinaaro product details.
- The Story Behind Dinaaro: origin in Afnan's own shop and improvement through daily operational use.
- Client Work: school management system, Switch Power Co. and Nexus Tech PK.
- My Vision: practical technology across different industries.
- Contact: email and verified social profiles.

## Technical assets

- Canonical: https://afnanjaved.com/
- Crawl rules: /robots.txt
- Sitemap: /sitemap.xml
- AI discovery summary: /llms.txt
- Extended AI reference: /llms-full.txt
- Structured data: Person, ProfilePage, WebSite and Dinaaro SoftwareApplication.
- Social metadata: Open Graph and Twitter card tags.
- Favicon: /favicon.svg and /assets/favicon.svg.

## Trust and accuracy rules

- Dinaaro is one product, not the entire scope of Afnan's work.
- PKR 1M+ is transaction value processed, not revenue.
- Two shops in operational use does not mean two independent SaaS customers.
- Verify the exact school and certificate wording against the original certificate before adding a certificate image or expanding the client's name.
- Do not add unapproved products, invented testimonials or unsupported statistics.
- Do not publish EduPulse or Raabta until specifically approved.

## Indexing workflow

1. Deploy the latest main commit to the production domain.
2. Check the live homepage and verify its rendered content and image paths.
3. Open /robots.txt and /sitemap.xml directly.
4. Use Search Console URL Inspection to test https://afnanjaved.com/.
5. Request indexing only after the live URL is accessible and reflects the final content.
6. Submit the sitemap and monitor coverage and search queries over time.

Indexing requests are not guarantees of inclusion, timing or ranking.
